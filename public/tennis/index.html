Data, definitions & limitations

Source: Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract, preserved by the Aneeshers archive. A fixed historical snapshot, not a live ATP feed. View source archive ↗

Completed singles at Grand Slams, Masters, main-tour and Tour Finals events. Team events, Olympics, Next Gen, retirements, defaults and walkovers are excluded. 2020 had a disrupted calendar. The archive has not been independently audited against every official result.

Win rate = wins / appearances. Serve and return percentages use sums of points, not averages of match percentages. Rankings are recorded at tournament start. Dates refer to tournament start, not the exact match day. Missing or inconsistent point statistics are excluded from rate denominators; results remain in match counts. Leaderboards require at least 20 completed matches in the selected scope.

These are descriptive comparisons, not causal estimates or match predictions. Players face different opponents and schedules. Source data and derived extracts: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, attribution to Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract; non-commercial use.
